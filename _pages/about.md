As a computational mathematician, I develop fast algorithms for densely structured linear and multilinear algebra problems. My current research focuses on developing tensor-based algorithms with an eye towards applications in scientific computing and machine learning; in particular, my focus is on efficiently computing kernel matrices.
 My broader vision is to develop efficient numerical methods that scale efficiently for higher problem sizes and mitigate the curse of dimensionality: the ultimate goal is for the complexity to grow linearly with the problem’s intrinsic dimension and overall size.
